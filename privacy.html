Privacy policy

Published by Muhammad Kashif Ali. Last updated 29 October 2026.

Shedload is an iPhone and iPad app for pricing and selling the things in your home. It has no accounts, no adverts, no analytics and no tracking. This page explains exactly what happens to your information.

What stays with you

Your photos, item details, prices, rooms, sales and settings are stored on your device. If iCloud is on, they're also stored in your own private iCloud, so they appear on your other devices. We can't see your iCloud data.

Pricing a photo

On devices with Apple Intelligence and iOS 27, Shedload prices photos on the device and the photo goes nowhere.

Otherwise, Shedload sends a smaller copy of the photo, and the name of the room you chose, to our server (run on Cloudflare). The server passes it to Anthropic's Claude API, which works out what the item is and what it might sell for, and sends the answer back. We don't store the photo. Anthropic handles it under its commercial terms and privacy policy.

Writing listings and eBay price checks

To write listings, Shedload sends the item's description (for example "Nintendo Switch Lite, good condition") to the same server and on to Claude, unless your device can write it on the device. To check prices, our server searches eBay UK for the item's name using eBay's public search. No photo or personal detail is sent to eBay. We keep search results for up to 24 hours so the same search isn't repeated.

What our server sees

Like any website, our server receives your IP address. We use it only to count requests, to stop anyone overloading the service. The count is held in memory, never written to storage, and resets daily. Cloudflare, which hosts the server, may keep standard logs under its own privacy policy.

Purchases

Subscriptions and listing packs are handled by Apple. We never see your payment details. Shedload keeps a count of your listing credits on your device and in your iCloud.

Email

If you email us from the app, the message includes your app version, iOS version and device model so we can help. Nothing is sent unless you tap Send. We use your email only to reply.

Children

Shedload isn't aimed at children and we don't knowingly handle children's information.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask what we hold about you and ask us to delete it. Apart from emails you send us, we don't hold anything that identifies you. To remove your Shedload data, delete the app and its iCloud data (Settings, your name, iCloud, Manage Account Storage, Shedload).

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top. Questions: shedload.help@outlook.com.
