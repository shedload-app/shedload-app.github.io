Help

Email shedload.help@outlook.com. We usually reply within two working days.

How accurate are the prices?

They're estimates, shown as a range. Shedload looks at the photo and, where it can, at similar used things for sale on eBay UK. For anything valuable, tap "See recent sold prices on eBay" on the item before you set a price.

Can Shedload post my listings for me?

No. Vinted, eBay and Facebook Marketplace don't let other apps post for you. Shedload writes the title, description and price, and you paste them into the marketplace app.

Why does it say it couldn't price a photo?

Usually the photo was blurry or showed several things at once. Photograph one thing at a time, fill the frame, and include any label or model number. Then open the item and tap "Try again".

Does it work without an internet connection?

On an iPhone or iPad with Apple Intelligence and iOS 27, photos are priced on the device, so yes. Otherwise Shedload needs a connection to price photos. Your catalogue always works offline.

How do I use it on my iPhone and iPad?

Sign in to the same Apple Account on both and make sure iCloud is on. Your catalogue, rooms and listing credits sync between them.

How do I cancel Pro?

Open Settings on your iPhone or iPad, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Shedload Pro, then Cancel Subscription. You keep Pro until the end of the period you've paid for.

I bought something and it isn't showing

In Shedload, tap Settings, then Restore purchases. Listing packs can't be restored by Apple, but Shedload keeps your credit count in your iCloud, so sign in to the same Apple Account.

How do I delete my data?

Delete the app, then open Settings, tap your name, then iCloud, then Manage Account Storage (or Manage Storage), then Shedload, and delete its data. Nothing about you is kept on our servers.
